## How it works

After a reset, the 7 segment display shows HELP cyclically

## How to test

Just reset the chip, clock the board (or do it manually) and verify that the 7-seg output is correct

## External hardware

There is a DIP switch that connects unused pins to VDD, the a reset button, and the 7-segment display.
